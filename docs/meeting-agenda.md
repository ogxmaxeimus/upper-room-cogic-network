# Meeting Agenda
## Upper Room COGIC Professional Network Demonstration

**Attendee:** Elder Anthony Wilson, President, Men’s Ministry  
**Presenter:** Brandon J. Fonville & Rufus Pritchett Jr.  
**Suggested duration:** 20 to 30 minutes  
**Format:** Brief presentation followed by live demonstration and discussion

---

### 1. Opening (2 minutes)

- Greeting and expression of appreciation for Elder Wilson’s leadership
- Statement of purpose: introduce the Upper Room COGIC Professional Network as a resource for the Men’s Ministry and the church

### 2. Problem and Opportunity (3 minutes)

- Many members have professional gifts that are not easily visible within the congregation
- Families and young men often need mentorship, services, and career connections
- The church needs a trusted, organized way to connect people with the right help

### 3. Solution Overview (3 minutes)

- A professional directory built specifically for Upper Room COGIC
- Members can be found by job type, specialty, location, and keyword search
- Profiles present skills, experience, availability, and professional background
- Connections happen through introduction requests with leadership visibility

### 4. Live Demonstration (10 minutes)

**Public site**
- Home page and featured members
- Search and browse by profession and city
- Sample member profile
- Join application form

**Admin panel**
- Coordinator login
- Pending applications queue
- Approve and publish workflow
- Manual member entry and profile editing

### 5. Ministry Alignment (3 minutes)

- Mentorship for young men
- Professional visibility for members
- Trusted referrals within the church
- Leadership oversight and quality control
- Potential expansion beyond the Men’s Ministry

### 6. Proposed Next Steps (3 minutes)

- Identify ministry coordinators for profile review
- Recruit an initial group of founding members
- Approve and publish first profiles
- Announce the directory to the Men’s Ministry
- Gather feedback and plan broader church rollout

### 7. Questions and Discussion (5 minutes)

- Elder Wilson’s feedback and concerns
- Suggested improvements before launch
- Agreement on whether to move forward with a pilot

### 8. Closing (1 minute)

- Thank Elder Wilson for his time and counsel
- Confirm follow up steps and timeline
