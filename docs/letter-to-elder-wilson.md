# Letter to Elder Anthony Wilson

**Subject:** Proposal to Introduce the Upper Room COGIC Professional Network

---

Elder Wilson,

I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to share a resource I have developed for our church community and to request your guidance as President of the Men’s Ministry at Upper Room COGIC.

Over the past several months, I have been building the **Upper Room COGIC Professional Network**, a professional directory designed to help members of our congregation discover, support, and connect with one another. Many people in our church possess valuable skills in business, trades, healthcare, law, technology, ministry, and other fields. However, we do not always have a structured way to make those gifts visible or accessible to the people who need them. This platform addresses that gap.

The network allows members to create professional profiles that include their name, job title, specialty, skills, years of experience, company, location, biography, portfolio or website, availability for hiring, freelance work, or mentoring, and optional scheduling links. Visitors can search the directory by name, skill, profession, or city. They can also browse by job type and specialty category. Each profile is designed to be clear, respectful, and easy to navigate.

From a ministry perspective, this is more than a website. It is a tool for fellowship, empowerment, and practical support. A young man seeking career direction can find a mentor within the church. A family in need of professional services can connect with a trusted member of our community. Entrepreneurs and skilled workers can represent their work with excellence while remaining connected to the body of Christ. The platform strengthens relationships while creating real opportunities for growth.

Leadership oversight is built into the system. Members apply through a join form. Applications are reviewed before any profile is published. Coordinators can approve, reject, or edit applications through a secure admin panel. They can also add members manually when appropriate. This ensures that the directory remains credible, current, and aligned with the standards of our church.

My hope is to present this resource first to the Men’s Ministry as a model for connection, mentorship, and professional fellowship. With your support, it can become a lasting asset for Upper Room COGIC and eventually serve the broader congregation.

I would be honored to meet with you at your convenience for a brief demonstration. I can walk you through the public directory, the application process, and the admin tools used to manage membership. I welcome your insight on how this may best serve our men, our families, and our church mission.

Thank you for your time, your leadership, and your service to Upper Room COGIC. I look forward to the opportunity to share this with you.

Respectfully,

Brandon J. Fonville & Rufus Pritchett Jr.
