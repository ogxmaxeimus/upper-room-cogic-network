### Purpose

The Upper Room COGIC Professional Network is a **professional and ministry directory** for our church. It helps members discover one another by career, specialty, and how they are available to serve, whether through mentorship, trusted services, or fellowship, within a space that reflects Upper Room’s identity and values.

### Who can join

Members of the Upper Room church family who wish to share their professional gifts in a respectful, leadership approved way. Participation is by **application**, not open self publishing.

### Approval flow

1. A person submits a **Join** application on the website.
2. Designated leadership (or coordinators) **review** the application in a password protected admin area.
3. Only **approved** profiles are published to the public directory.
4. Visitors may **search**, view profiles, and request a **privacy minded introduction**, so connections stay intentional without broadcasting private contact details.
