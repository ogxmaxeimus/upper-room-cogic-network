# Coordinator Responsibilities
## Upper Room COGIC Professional Network

**Prepared by:** Brandon J. Fonville & Rufus Pritchett Jr.

This document outlines the responsibilities of ministry coordinators who manage the professional network on behalf of church leadership.

---

### Purpose

Coordinators serve as trusted gatekeepers for the directory. They ensure that published profiles are accurate, appropriate, and representative of the standards of Upper Room COGIC.

---

### Core Responsibilities

**1. Review join applications**
- Check that submitted information is complete and professional
- Confirm the applicant is connected to the church community
- Approve, reject, or return applications for revision

**2. Publish approved members**
- Use the admin panel to approve applications and publish profiles
- Edit profile details when necessary before publication
- Assign verified status to trusted members

**3. Maintain the directory**
- Update outdated job titles, locations, and contact preferences
- Remove profiles when members request removal or are no longer active
- Add members manually when leadership approves direct entry

**4. Monitor connection requests**
- Review introduction requests submitted through the site
- Help facilitate appropriate connections between members
- Protect member privacy and church reputation

**5. Support ministry rollout**
- Encourage qualified men and women to join the network
- Recommend featured members when appropriate
- Report issues, feedback, and improvement ideas to ministry leadership

---

### Approval Standards

Profiles should be approved when they meet the following expectations:

- The member is affiliated with Upper Room COGIC or approved church leadership
- Information is truthful and professionally presented
- Biography and links are appropriate for a church audience
- Availability tags accurately reflect how the member wants to be contacted
- No misleading claims, offensive content, or inappropriate material

Applications should be rejected or held for revision when:

- Information is incomplete or unverifiable
- Content is inappropriate or inconsistent with church values
- The applicant cannot be confirmed as part of the church community
- The profile appears promotional in a way that harms credibility

---

### Suggested Review Cadence

- **Pending applications:** reviewed within 3 to 5 business days
- **Directory audit:** monthly review for outdated profiles
- **Featured members:** updated quarterly or as leadership directs

---

### Access and Security

- Coordinators receive admin login credentials from ministry leadership
- Passwords should be changed from the default before launch
- Admin access should be limited to approved coordinators only
- Coordinators should not share login credentials

---

### Accountability

Coordinators serve under the oversight of the Men’s Ministry leadership and church administration. Questions about policy, disputes, or sensitive profile decisions should be elevated to Elder Wilson or designated church leadership.
