# Upper Room COGIC Professional Network
## One Page Summary for Leadership

**Prepared for:** Elder Anthony Wilson, President, Men’s Ministry  
**Organization:** Upper Room Church of God in Christ  
**Document purpose:** Overview of the professional networking platform and proposed rollout

---

### What It Is

The Upper Room COGIC Professional Network is a church based professional directory that helps members discover one another by career, specialty, and location. It creates a trusted space where believers can share their skills, offer mentorship, provide services, and build meaningful connections within the congregation.

### The Need It Addresses

Many members of our church are accomplished professionals, skilled tradespeople, entrepreneurs, and mentors. Yet there is no centralized way to find them when help, guidance, or collaboration is needed. This platform organizes that knowledge so the church can better steward the gifts already present in our community.

### How It Works

1. **Members apply** through a join form on the website.
2. **Leadership reviews** each application in the admin panel.
3. **Approved profiles are published** to the public directory.
4. **Visitors search and browse** by profession, specialty, city, or keyword.
5. **Connections are made** through introduction requests rather than public exposure of private contact information.

### Key Features

| Feature | Benefit |
|--------|---------|
| Searchable member directory | Find the right person quickly |
| Job types and specialty categories | Easy browsing by profession |
| City based browsing | Connect with members in your area |
| Member profiles | Name, title, skills, experience, bio, links, availability |
| Verified member status | Builds trust within the church community |
| Featured members | Highlight impactful professionals |
| Mentorship and hiring tags | Clarify how each member is available to serve |
| Join application form | Controlled onboarding for new members |
| Admin approval workflow | Leadership maintains quality and oversight |
| Manual member management | Coordinators can add or update profiles directly |

### Ministry Value

- Supports **mentorship** for young men and emerging professionals
- Encourages **economic empowerment** through visibility and referrals
- Strengthens **fellowship** across careers and life stages
- Provides a **trusted alternative** to searching outside the church community
- Reflects **excellence, integrity, and service** in how members present their work

### Leadership Oversight

The platform includes a coordinator admin panel where authorized leadership can:

- Review pending applications
- Approve or reject join requests
- Edit profile details before publishing
- Add members manually
- Remove outdated profiles
- Monitor contact and introduction requests

This ensures the directory remains accurate, respectful, and aligned with church leadership.

### Proposed Rollout

**Phase 1:** Present to Men’s Ministry leadership and identify coordinators  
**Phase 2:** Invite an initial group of men to submit profiles  
**Phase 3:** Review, approve, and publish founding members  
**Phase 4:** Announce the directory to the congregation  
**Phase 5:** Expand participation across ministries and the broader church
