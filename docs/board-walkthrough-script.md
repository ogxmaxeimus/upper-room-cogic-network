# Executive Board Walkthrough Script

**Formats:** 8 to 12 minute live demo **or** 3 to 5 minute recorded overview

## 1. Home: What it is (~1.5 to 2 min)

**On screen:** Home page (logo, brand, featured members)

**Say:**

> Elder Wilson, thank you for your time. We’ve prepared a brief walkthrough of the **Upper Room COGIC Professional Network**.
>
> This is a **professional and ministry directory** for our church. Its purpose is simple: help members of Upper Room find one another by career, specialty, and how they’re available to serve, through mentorship, services, or fellowship.
>
> The look and feel uses a warm brown and cream theme and the **official Upper Room logo**, so it reads as a church resource, not a generic social network.
>
> Today the site is live with a small founding group of real members. We will await **on leadership approval** before we invite a wider group to join. That way growth stays under board guidance.

**Optional aside (live only):** Point briefly at the logo and one featured name so the board sees it is already real, not a mockup.

## 2. Search: Find by name or specialty (~1.5 to 2 min)

**On screen:** Search / directory browse

**Say:**

> From here, anyone can **search by name** or look for a **specialty**, for example a trade, a profession, or a skill the church family might need.
>
> The goal is not to replace personal relationships. It’s to make gifts that are already in the congregation **easier to find** when someone needs help, guidance, or a trusted referral inside Upper Room.

**Demo tip:** Search a known name (e.g. “Wooden,” “Raeford,” “Fonville,” or “Little”) or a specialty such as HVAC so results appear quickly.

## 3. Member profile: Real example and privacy (~2 to 2.5 min)

**On screen:** Open one live profile (recommend Damian Little for a clear specialty, or Bishop Wooden for ministry leadership presence)

**Say:**

> Here’s a **real member profile** on the site today. You’ll see professional background, specialty, and how that person is available to connect.
>
> Contact is handled carefully. Rather than putting private phone numbers and emails out in the open, the directory supports **privacy minded introductions**. Someone can request an introduction through the site so connections stay respectful and intentional.
>
> That design choice matters for ministry: we want visibility for service and fellowship, without unnecessary exposure of personal information.

**Live members we may mention if asked (all real, on the site today):**

1. Bishop Patrick L. Wooden Sr.
2. Clarence “Rocky” Raeford
3. Brandon J. Fonville & Rufus Pritchett Jr.
4. Damian Little (HVAC / Temperature Pros LLC)



## 4. Join: How people apply (~1.5 to 2 min)

**On screen:** Join / application form

**Say:**

> When we are ready to grow the directory, people don’t publish themselves overnight. They **apply through the Join form**.
>
> Leadership, or designated coordinators, **review each application** before a profile goes live. That keeps the directory accurate, trustworthy, and aligned with church standards.
>
> Right now we are not actively recruiting beyond the founding members. Expanding the roster is exactly what I’m asking the board to consider.



## 5. Admin: Leadership only overview (~1.5 to 2 min)

**On screen:** Admin login page only (or describe without logging in). **Password is subject to change if board deems neccessary.** 

**Say:**

> Behind the scenes there is a **password protected admin area** for leadership and trusted coordinators only.
>
> From there, authorized people can **review applications**, approve or decline them, and keep the directory current. Access would be shared privately with whoever the board designates.
>
> The important point for the board is: **oversight is built in**. This is not an open free for all. Publication stays under leadership control.

**If pressed for a deeper demo:** Offer a private follow up with designated coordinators only. Still do not put the password in slides, handouts, or recordings.

## 6. Close: Status today and the ask (~1.5 to 2 min)

**On screen:** Return to Home

**Say:**

> **Where we are today:** The site is live. Real founding members are listed. Search, profiles, Join applications, admin review, and privacy minded introductions are in place. The brand reflects Upper Room.
>
> **What we are waiting on:** Leadership approval to invite more members and grow this as a church resource.
>
> **What we are asking of the board:** Review and affirm this as an Upper Room ministry tool; give guidance on who should review applications; and approve a next step to carefully invite additional members under your oversight.
>
> We're grateful for your leadership and happy to answer questions.

**Then stop talking** and take questions.
