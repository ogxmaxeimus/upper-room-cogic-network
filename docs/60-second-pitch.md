# 60 Second Spoken Pitch
## For Elder Anthony Wilson

**Presenter:** Brandon J. Fonville & Rufus Pritchett Jr.

---

Elder Wilson, thank you for your time.

I have developed a professional networking platform for Upper Room COGIC called the Upper Room Professional Network. Its purpose is to help members of our church discover one another by career, specialty, and location.

Many people in our congregation have strong professional gifts, but we do not always know who they are or how to connect with them. This directory solves that problem. Members can create profiles, share their skills, and make themselves available for mentorship, hiring, or freelance work. Visitors can search by profession, browse by city, and request an introduction through the site.

What makes this valuable for ministry is that it is built with leadership oversight. People apply to join, leadership reviews each application, and only approved profiles are published. That keeps the directory trustworthy and aligned with our church standards.

I believe this can be a strong resource for the Men’s Ministry. It supports mentorship, professional fellowship, and practical help within our own community. I would be honored to give you a brief demonstration and hear your thoughts on how we might introduce it to the men of Upper Room.

Thank you again for your leadership.

---

**Estimated delivery time:** 55 to 65 seconds at a natural speaking pace
